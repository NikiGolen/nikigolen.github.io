Experiment 04

Query-Time Schema Fallback (QSF)

A middleware prototype for recovering missing product attributes at query time instead of just dropping them.

When a boolean flag like isWireless comes back null, QSF falls back to tags like #corded or #wired to resolve it, and traces every step of that decision live instead of burying it in a console log.
